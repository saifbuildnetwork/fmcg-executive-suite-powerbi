# 📊 FMCG Executive Suite — Power BI Analytics Dashboard

Enterprise-grade Power BI analytical suite designed for C-suite and FMCG leadership to evaluate revenue velocity, supply chain resiliency, inventory holding costs, and granular SKU-level demand elasticity.

---

## 🚀 Key Dashboard Viewports & Features

### 1. Executive Overview
Focuses on macro-level business performance, revenue trends, category distribution, and channel breakdown.
* **Core KPIs**: Total Revenue, Average Order Value (AOV), Total Quantity Sold, Total Orders.
* **Visual Insights**: Monthly revenue progression line chart and channel share breakdown (Retail, E-commerce, Discount).

![Executive Overview](Screenshot%202026-10-25%20113955.png)

---

### 2. Sales & Inventory Analysis
Deep-dives into operational sales metrics, promotional efficacy, stock availability, and supply chain logistics.
* **Core KPIs**: Promo Sales %, Avg Delivery Days, Avg Stock Available, Total Units Sold.
* **Visual Insights**: Top 5 vs. Bottom 5 SKUs by volume, channel distribution, and Stock vs. Units Sold correlation analysis.

![Sales & Inventory Analysis](Screenshot%202026-10-25%20114011.png)

---

### 3. SKU Deep-Dive & Macro Drivers
Granular analysis tracking weekly sales momentum, temporal demand lag, and target forecasts.
* **Visual Insights**: Weekly Sales Acceleration/Deceleration analysis, 4-Week Rolling Mean trends, and Actual Sales vs. Forecasted Demand matching.

![SKU Deep-Dive & Macro Drivers](Screenshot%202026-10-25%20114024.png)
